## Full description
Decode the Logic, Crack the Code!

Welcome to CryptiQ, the ultimate cryptarithm math puzzle game where words become numbers and logic is your only weapon. If you love Sudoku, Nonograms, or classic brain teasers, CryptiQ will push your deductive reasoning to the absolute limit!

In a cryptarithm (also known as an alphametic puzzle), letters represent unique digits. Your goal is to figure out which letter stands for which number to make the mathematical equation correct. From the classic `SEND + MORE = MONEY` to 1,200 mind-bending levels, every puzzle is a fresh challenge.

🔥 ALL LEVELS ARE 100% FREE!
We believe in pure gameplay. Every level unlocks just by playing — no paywalls and no subscriptions. CryptiQ Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks all levels instantly.

🧠 FEATURES THAT MAKE CRYPTIQ UNIQUE:
• 1,200 Levels: Addition, subtraction, multiplication and multi-step chain puzzles, from Easy to Expert.
• Daily Challenge: A new puzzle every day. Build your streak 🔥 and beat your own best time!
• Multiplayer (Pass & Play): Challenge your friends in local multiplayer mode! Compete to see who can crack the code first.
• Beautiful Glassmorphism UI: Experience a sleek, premium dark-mode aesthetic with satisfying micro-animations that make solving puzzles a visual treat.
• Smart Hint System: Stuck on a difficult equation? Use hints to reveal a single letter and get back on track.
• Progress Tracking: Monitor your stats, best times, and overall logic mastery.

🎯 HOW TO PLAY:
1. Each letter represents a unique number from 0 to 9.
2. The same letter always represents the same number.
3. Determine the correct digits to make the mathematical equation mathematically true.
4. No numbers can start with a zero (e.g., 085 is invalid).
