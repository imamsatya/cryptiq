Privacy Policy for CryptiQ

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CryptiQ ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected through your use of the CryptiQ mobile application (the "App").

CryptiQ does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) on our servers.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. Local Data Storage

All of your game progress, settings, and statistics are stored locally on your device using a local database. This data never leaves your device unless it is backed up by your operating system's native cloud backup service (e.g., Google Drive or iCloud) according to your personal device settings.

B. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily for advertising and analytics purposes.

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may use device identifiers and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA) to personalize ads and analyze their performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's privacy settings.
  • In-App Purchases: We offer in-app purchases processed entirely by the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not have access to, nor do we collect, your credit card information or billing details.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is safe for general audiences, but we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data for us to delete on our end, but you may clear the App's local data from your device settings.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
